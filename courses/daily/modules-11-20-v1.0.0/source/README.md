### CD-0011

Source and verification inventory (full section 7):

**Actual supplied media assets:** None.

**Suggested optional media:** An independently created sample coverage statement with labels identifying area, quantity, and “per coat.” Mark it “Illustrative example—not a product specification.”

**Accessibility description for proposed media:** “A fictional coverage statement reads ‘300–400 square feet per gallon, per coat.’ Separate labels identify the surface-area range, paint quantity, and one-coat basis.” Provide all wording as readable text.

**Sources checked October 22, 2026:**

- [Source 1] BEHR, [How Much Interior Paint to Buy](https://www.behr.com/how-to/interior/determine-how-much-interior-paint-to-buy): one-coat surface coverage and factors that may require additional paint or coats.
- [Source 2] Sherwin-Williams, [Paint Calculator](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/homeowners/color/color-tools/paint-calculator): approximate estimates and variation with application method and surface condition.
- [Source 3] Manufacturer technical data sheet, [Magnolia Home Interior Matte Paint](https://www.kilz.com/binaries/content/assets/magnolia/pdf/tds/tds-premiuim-interior-matte-paint.pdf), “Coverage Rate”: texture, porosity, and application method as coverage factors. Used for these general principles, not as a current specification for a store’s product.
- [Source 4] Sherwin-Williams, [How to Fix Poor Hiding](https://ltx-www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/project-center/maintenance-repair/how-to-fix-poor-hiding): hiding as concealment of the underlying color or surface.

**Verification needs:** The scenario’s coverage range is fictional and explicitly stated per coat. Verify the exact current label or technical data sheet before substituting a real product. Do not assume every manufacturer expresses coverage on the same basis.
